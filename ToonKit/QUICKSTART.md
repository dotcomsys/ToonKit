# ToonKit quick start

ToonKit supports the WoW Forever client. The version beside the journal title identifies the copy loaded by the game. Editing the development workspace does not change that copy.

## Continue leveling

1. Type `/tk` and open **Journey**. Choose **Questing**, **Questing + dungeons**, or **Solo speedrun**. The first two share progress and have different dungeon defaults. The solo campaign has separate progress.
2. Choose **Find my next route** to join at your current level, or open **Leveling campaign** and choose a segment. Keep automatic advancement on to follow the linked campaign as you complete its steps.
3. Follow the current action and use **Map** when there is a destination. If the guide shows a prerequisite, do that first. **Skip task** leaves work unfinished. **Undo** restores the last supported manual change.
4. Use **Guide tools** for preparation, quest history, route timings, and retracking. The floating guide also has a Guide tools menu. Direction notes, next actions and progress are configurable under **Settings → Advanced settings → Journey → Guide**.
5. The watched-quest tracker follows Blizzard’s quest watches. Click a title for native quest details or right-click for options. Focusing a watched quest temporarily redirects the arrow. Choose **Resume route** on the floating guide to return.

## Use bags and compare equipment

- Use **Open bags** or `/tk bags` to see carried items. Use the character button to browse recorded bags. Those snapshots are read-only.
- Search `ilvl:>=40` for equipment item level or `level:<=48` for required character level. Hover materials for confirmed learned recipes and general uses. Recipe rows distinguish the sourced minimum skill, the character's current skill and whether learning is confirmed. Shift-hover for a longer recipe list.
- Open **Equipment**, choose a slot and select a result to see its source and comparison. **Track** records an acquisition target. **Start hunt** follows it when a route is available. **Talent plans** opens saved and native talent guidance.
- The bank uses Blizzard’s frame. In a bulk review, check the exact stacks and exclusions before confirming. Ordinary item click and drag controls still use the native item templates.

## Find a dungeon quest or a book

- In **Dungeons**, enter a dungeon, boss, quest, item name, or exact ID in the search field and press Enter. Select a result to open the matching quest or boss. Alliance, Horde and class icons appear beside readable names. Incompatible quests have their own group.
- **Include in Journey** and **Leave out of Journey** control dungeon preparation for that quest in the questing campaign. Native quest tracking is separate.
- **Map** lists sourced interior floors when available. **Entrance area** opens the outdoor approach.
- Open **Books** in the journal or use `/tk world`. Search the collection and select a zone and book for its progress, source, and recorded locations. **Milestone rewards** shows the donation rewards. **Browse on map** opens the optional map list, where **Back to journal** restores the collection page.

## Change settings or report a problem

- The status bar’s right-click action and `/tk xp` open **Status bar** settings directly. Use `/tk bar` to toggle the bar.
- Press **M → ToonKit layers** to choose map overlays. The optional seamless map integration depends on a compatible installed provider.
- At the auction house, **ToonKit prices** observes current asking prices. An observation can be partial or time out. Purchases and postings use the native auction interface.
- Use `/tk diagnostics` to copy a local report. It includes the running UI revision. Note the character, quest or item, and the visible action that led to the issue.
